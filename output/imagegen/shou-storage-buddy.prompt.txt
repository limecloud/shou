Use case: logo-brand
Asset type: the primary app icon and brand mascot for Shou, a native desktop disk-cleaning and organizing utility.
Primary request: Create one original, lovable object mascot: a little rounded storage box that feels like a helpful tidy companion. A distinctive, simple silhouette and expressive face should make it memorable at very small sizes. Refined enough for a premium desktop application, warm and cute enough to become a recognizable consumer brand.
Subject: A substantial softly rounded teal storage box, viewed almost from the front and slightly from above. A thick warm ivory lid is tilted slightly open, showing a small dark teal opening. Two large, well-spaced dark ink oval eyes and a short, confident U-shaped smile are part of the box's front face. A single small coral pull tab on the lid provides a recognizable accent. The box itself is the entire character; no animal, no ears, no human body, no hands, no legs.
Style/medium: Polished soft 3D product illustration with a restrained ceramic and soft-touch vinyl finish. Bold clean geometry, tactile bevels, carefully placed highlights, subtle ambient shadows. Keep the facial features simple and strongly readable, not a detailed toy scene.
Composition/framing: A single centered mascot, filling about 74 percent of the square image, with balanced breathing room on all sides. Strong front-facing silhouette, minimal perspective, no props, no surrounding objects. Full-bleed square pale mint-to-warm-ivory background; no rounded-square tile, no icon frame, no borders. The production pipeline will apply the native app icon mask.
Lighting/mood: Soft studio light from upper left, quietly joyful, reassuring and friendly. A restrained grounding shadow directly under the character.
Color palette: Rich jade teal body, deeper teal side shading, warm ivory lid, a small coral accent, dark ink eyes and mouth. Avoid washed-out pastel contrast.
Text: None.
Constraints: Entirely original identity. No existing company mascots, trademarks or logos. No letters, words, arrows, check marks, sparkles, broom, trash-can symbol, screens, disks, badges or watermark. Do not create an icon grid or a presentation sheet. One finished icon composition only.
